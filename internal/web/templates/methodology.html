Method {{.Methodology}}

Measurements that can be challenged.

StratumStats publishes observed dimensions separately. It does not collapse subjective weights into a composite score or declare one universally best pool.

Published measurements

Availability

Conservative 95% Wilson lower bound of full-template arrivals over eligible blocks. Disappearing during an observed block counts as a miss.

Median delivery

Median full-template delay relative to the earliest structurally valid template observed for the same block and vantage.

Tail delivery

95th-percentile relative delay, exposing intermittent stalls that a median can hide.

Transport security

Whether a configured TLS endpoint successfully completed a measured Stratum session.

Why empty-first is not scored

After a new block, Bitcoin Core validates the block, updates chain and mempool state, and assembles a transaction-bearing template. Sending coinbase-only work immediately can prevent miners from wasting time on the stale parent. Empty-first frequency is not inherently bad; only empty-to-full duration and resulting fee opportunity cost are meaningful.

Payout custody and pool fee

For every structurally valid job, the probe reconstructs the coinbase with its negotiated extranonces and looks for the exact generated worker payout script. A match is direct coinbase payment. No match means the miner must trust the pool to account for and later pay earnings.

For direct payment, the observed pool fee is 100 × (all coinbase outputs − worker outputs) / all coinbase outputs. Optional donations or configured splits can be included, so this is the effective fee visible in the work—not a claim about the pool’s advertised policy. A fee cannot be inferred for custodial payouts from Stratum alone.

What is deliberately excluded

Pool size, popularity, advertising, sponsorship, subjective reputation, unverified operator claims, and any composite score. Custodial payout correctness still needs a controlled hashrate/payment study.

Anonymity and its limit

Each connection uses a newly generated valid-looking Bitcoin address, ordinary worker name, and rotated miner agent. Credentials are never persisted. This reduces special treatment based on a known benchmark account, but it does not conceal the probe’s source IP. Published vantage labels should be coarse regions, never hostnames or IP addresses.
